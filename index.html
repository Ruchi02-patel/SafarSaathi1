Smart Vehicle Management Features

Designed for individual vehicle owners and commercial fleet operators.

🚗

Garage & Vehicle Registry

Store 2-wheelers, 4-wheelers, and commercial vehicles with chassis number, fuel type, and registration specifications.

📁

Digital Document Vault

Securely manage RC book, driving licence, insurance certificate, and PUC documents with expiry reminders.

⛽

Fuel & Mileage Tracker

Log fuel quantity, cost, and odometer readings. Automatic distance and mileage calculation (km/litre).

Service & Maintenance

Log periodic servicing, oil changes, tyre replacements, and brake maintenance with automated due date countdowns.

Expense Analytics

Comprehensive expenditure breakdown across fuel, maintenance, insurance, and repairs with monthly summaries.

Insurance & EMI Calculator
