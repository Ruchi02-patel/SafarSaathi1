📚 Viva Presentation Key Rules & Explanations (Motor Vehicles Act)

1. Section 41 (7) MV Act Registration of a private motor car is valid for an initial period of 15 years from the date of issuance and must be renewed every 5 years thereafter.
2. Green Tax Provisions Central Motor Vehicles Rules mandate Green Tax on personal vehicles at 10-25% of road tax at 15-year renewal. Electric and CNG vehicles are strictly exempt.
3. Delhi NCR Special Rule Under NGT and Supreme Court orders, diesel vehicles > 10 years and petrol vehicles > 15 years cannot ply on public roads in the National Capital Region.
